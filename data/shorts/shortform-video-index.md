## Short-Form Strategy & Host Pillars

### Core Strategic Insights

**What Works for ragTech:**
- **Skit/Funny format**: Massively outperforms all other formats (72K avg plays). Our engineering humor resonates strongly.
- **Original reporting**: Investigative angles like Malaysia data center series drive 30K+ avg plays and high engagement.
- **Contrarian opinions**: Hot takes and "you've been punked" framing consistently perform well.
- **Multi-host chemistry**: Videos with 2+ hosts show higher engagement, especially in skit format.

**What to Avoid:**
- **Pure tutorials**: Underperform significantly without strong narrative hooks.
- **Product demos**: Behind-the-scenes content barely reaches beyond existing audience.
- **Technical explainers**: LLM fundamentals and concept breakdowns have lowest reach.
- **Guest content**: Performs poorly without strong hooks or host integration.

### Content Strategy Framework

**Weekly Content Mix:**
- **2 Skit/Funny videos** (Natasha-led, multi-host when possible)
- **1 Original Reporting piece** (Natasha investigative angle)
- **1 Contrarian Opinion** (Any host, strong hook required)
- **1 Technical Deep Dive** (Victoria, with practical consequences)
- **1 Policy/Industry Analysis** (Saloni, regional relevance)

**Hook Optimization Rules:**
1. **Lead with consequence**: "X will cause Y" vs "Here's how X works"
2. **Use intrigue**: "The dangerous myth of..." vs "Understanding AI agents"
3. **Personal vulnerability**: "When I get depressed about..." vs "How to handle interview prep"
4. **Contrarian framing**: "Why everyone is wrong about..." vs "The truth about..."

**Platform Strategy:**
- **Instagram Reels**: Primary platform, focus on viral formats
- **TikTok**: Repurpose top-performing Instagram content
- **YouTube Shorts**: Technical explainers for niche audience

**Success Metrics:**
- **Viral threshold**: 10K+ plays (Tier 1)
- **Strong performance**: 3K-10K plays (Tier 2)
- **Engagement quality**: Comments-to-plays ratio > 0.5%
- **Share velocity**: First 24 hours performance predicts long-term reach
