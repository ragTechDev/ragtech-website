| **Topic** | The tech concept covered (e.g. imposter syndrome, AI hype, CDN, burnout) |

## Engagement Signal Guide

| Signal | Platform | Strategic meaning |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Saves** | Instagram | Strongest value signal — "I want to return to this." High-save topics should become episodes or blog posts. |
| **Shares** | TikTok | Primary growth driver. High shares = broad relatability. |
| **Reach vs Likes ratio** | Instagram | Low reach + high likes = strong content, weak distribution. High reach + low likes = weak hook or wrong audience. |
| **Loop rate** | TikTok | High loops = visually engaging or punchline rewards re-reading. Good signal for comics specifically. |
| **Tags in comments** | All | Strongest organic growth signal — note which topics trigger this. |
