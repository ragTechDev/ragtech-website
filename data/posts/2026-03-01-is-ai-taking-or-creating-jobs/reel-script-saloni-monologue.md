# Reel Script: Saloni Monologue
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok
**Host:** Saloni (solo)
**Target length:** ~75-90 seconds
**Format:** Monologue, storytelling, direct to camera

---

## SCRIPT

**[0:00 - 0:10 | HOOK]**
So a friend of mine sent me a message the other day.
She's a software developer, five years of experience.
And she said: "Saloni, am I going to lose my job to AI?"

That question actually scared me a little too.
Because the answer is not simple.

---

**[0:10 - 0:28 | BOTH SIDES]**
Because here's the thing.
AI IS replacing certain roles.
Data labelling, basic code reviews, entry-level support work.
One engineer with Cursor or Copilot today can do what used to take a whole junior team.
That part is real. I've seen it happen.

And here's the scary part: companies are laying people off not because AI has already replaced them,
but because they THINK it will. The fear is moving faster than the reality.

But at the same time?
Demand for AI engineers has actually gone up 25%.
Companies that are actually building AI products, not just using them,
need ML Engineers, AI Engineers, Data Scientists to build and maintain these systems.
Those roles are in demand. And there are more of them than people realise.

So yes. AND no.
And the tech community is the most caught in the middle of this.

---

**[0:28 - 0:48 | THE HIDDEN PROBLEM]**
But here's what nobody is talking about.
Even the new jobs being created? They're incredibly hard to find.

About 30% of job postings right now are ghost jobs.
Roles companies post but aren't actually hiring for.
So you're applying, customising your resume, going through rounds...
and the role was never real to begin with.

And even if you're genuinely strong, solid GitHub, real experience,
most platforms just filter you by keywords.
Your actual work doesn't matter.
You're just a resume in a pile of thousands.

This is the reality of the job market in 2026.
And it is kind of terrifying.

---

**[0:48 - 1:10 | FONZI + MATCH DAY]**
So I came across this platform called Fonzi.
[show fonzi.ai on screen]
And what they do completely flips the dynamic.

They vet the engineers. They vet the companies.
And then they run something called Match Day.

Instead of you applying to 50 companies and hearing nothing back,
companies come to YOU with salary-backed offers.
You see who's interested. You pick who you want to talk to.
No 10-round interview loops. No ghosting.

One engineer before Fonzi: seven months, 119 interviews.
With Fonzi: a signed offer in 2 to 3 weeks.

---

**[1:10 - 1:22 | CTA]**
Link is in my bio if you want to check it out.

But I also want to know: has the job market felt like this to you?
Are you seeing this too?
Drop a comment, because I think we need to talk about this more.

---

## Production Notes

- **Open with the friend's message** as a prop if possible: show phone screen with the WhatsApp/text, then look up to camera
- **"Yes. AND no."** is a beat: pause before the "AND no" for effect
- **Ghost jobs stat + "30%"** as text overlay on screen
- **"This is the reality of the job market in 2026"** is a beat: pause, let it land
- **Fonzi section**: show `fonzi.ai` on screen clearly (top or middle, 100% visible) while narrating Match Day
- **Screen recording**: show the talent.fonzi.ai application flow if possible as B-roll
- **Tone:** Not a sales pitch. Saloni is sharing something she genuinely found, like she would to a friend. The scary reality section should feel real and relatable, not performative.
- The goal is to drive comments: "has this happened to you?", "is this your experience?" — the audience should feel seen
- Keep delivery loose, not word-for-word. The beats matter more than exact lines.
- Do NOT call Fonzi an "AI Recruiter" on screen or in caption
