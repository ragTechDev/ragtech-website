# Reel Script: Saloni Monologue
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok
**Host:** Saloni (solo)
**Target length:** ~75-90 seconds
**Format:** Monologue, storytelling, direct to camera

---

## SCRIPT

**[0:00 - 0:10 | HOOK]**
So a friend of mine sent me a message the other day.
She's a software developer, five years of experience.
And she said: "Saloni, am I going to lose my job to AI?"

And I had to be honest with her.
I said: it depends.

---

**[0:10 - 0:28 | BOTH SIDES]**
Because here's the thing.
AI IS replacing certain roles.
Data labelling, basic code reviews, entry-level support work.
One engineer with Cursor or Copilot today can do what used to take a whole junior team.
That part is real. I've seen it happen.

But at the same time?
Demand for AI engineers has gone up by 25%.
Every single company right now needs people who can actually BUILD these systems.
ML Engineers, AI Engineers, Data Scientists.
The jobs are there.

So the answer to "is AI taking jobs" is: yes. AND no.
The tech community is the most affected, both ways.

---

**[0:28 - 0:48 | THE HIDDEN PROBLEM]**
But here's what nobody talks about.
Even the new jobs being created? They're really hard to find.

Because the job market right now is so noisy.
About 30% of job postings in 2026 are ghost jobs.
Roles companies post but aren't actually hiring for.
So you're applying, customising your resume, going through processes...
and the job was never real.

And even if you're a strong candidate, like genuinely good,
most platforms just filter you by keywords.
Your GitHub doesn't matter. Your actual work doesn't matter.
You're just a resume in a pile.

---

**[0:48 - 1:10 | FONZI + MATCH DAY]**
So I came across this platform called Fonzi.
And what they do is actually quite different.

They vet the engineers. They vet the companies.
And then once a month, they run something called Match Day.

So instead of you applying to 50 companies and hearing nothing,
companies come to you with actual salary-backed offers.
In 48 hours.
You just look at who's interested, and you pick who you want to talk to.

One engineer said he spent seven months, 119 interviews,
before finding the right role the old way.
With Fonzi? First call to first day in two weeks.

---

**[1:10 - 1:18 | CTA]**
Link is in my bio if you want to check it out.

And if you're an engineer navigating this market right now,
I genuinely hope this helps.
Because it should not be this hard.

---

## Production Notes

- **Open with the friend's message** as a prop if possible: show phone screen with the WhatsApp/text, then look up to camera
- **"Yes. AND no."** is a beat: pause before the "AND no" for effect
- **Ghost jobs stat** can be a text overlay on screen
- **Match Day section**: consider cutting to a quick screen recording of fonzi.ai as B-roll while Saloni narrates
- **Tone:** Saloni is not doing a sales pitch. She's telling a story about something she genuinely found and is sharing it like she would with a friend. Warm but matter-of-fact.
- Keep delivery loose, not word-for-word. The beats matter more than the exact lines.
