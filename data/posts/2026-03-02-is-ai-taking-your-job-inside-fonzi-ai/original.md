Here's a question we keep hearing: is AI taking our jobs?

Ask around and you get two camps. Some people say yes, absolutely. Others say we're asking the wrong question entirely.

The honest answer is that both sides have a point. AI is replacing some roles, creating others, and making the job market harder to navigate than it's ever been. Engineers, especially those working in or around AI, are caught right in the middle of this.

We wanted to dig into this properly. That research led us to [Fonzi.ai](https://fonzi.ai/), a platform built to help engineers find their footing in this new landscape.

## The Case That AI Is Taking Jobs

There are roles that AI has already made redundant, or will soon. Junior copywriting, basic data labelling, routine code review, entry-level customer support: AI tools are doing these tasks faster and cheaper than humans, and companies have noticed.

For engineers, the story isn't entirely rosy either. Tools like Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and Claude mean one engineer today can do the work that previously required three. Teams are shipping faster with smaller headcounts. Some engineering roles, especially at smaller startups, are being deferred or cut entirely because a non-technical founder with the right AI tools can now get far enough on their own.

We've seen this at startup pitch nights in Singapore, where more and more founders are building MVPs solo with no CTO in sight. We actually wrote about this in our [CTO piece](/blog/why-finding-a-cto-is-harder-than-you-think). The pressure on traditional engineering roles is real.

## The Case That AI Is Creating Jobs

At the same time, the demand for engineers is going up, not down. Every AI product needs someone to train it, fine-tune it, deploy it, and keep it running. Every company adding AI to their workflow needs ML Engineers, Data Scientists, AI Platform Engineers, and Fullstack Engineers to build the interfaces that bring these systems to users. The AI boom isn't just creating AI companies. It's adding AI functions to every kind of company, in every industry.

According to Fonzi's data, there's been a **25% surge in AI talent demand**, and that number keeps climbing. The supply of qualified engineers hasn't kept pace. Senior ML Engineers and AI Research Scientists are rare. Companies also need people who understand both what AI can do and what it can't, which is a harder profile to find than a standard engineering hire.

So yes, AI is creating jobs. Lots of them. The problem is that finding those jobs, and being found for them, is harder than it should be.

## The Real Problem: The Job Market Itself Is Broken

The job search experience for engineers in 2026 is, by many measures, worse than it was five years ago.

Research suggests roughly **30% of job postings in 2026 are "ghost jobs"**: positions companies post but aren't actively filling. They might be building a talent pipeline, fulfilling internal processes, or testing market salaries. Whatever the reason, engineers spend real time applying to roles that were never real opportunities.

For strong candidates, there's also a visibility problem. If you're a senior ML Engineer with a solid GitHub, good experience, and clear results, how do you stand out? Most job boards reduce you to keywords and checkboxes. Automated filtering systems can make an impressive resume look identical to a mediocre one.

On the company side, it's just as frustrating. Every job posting gets flooded with applicants, many of whom overstate their experience. Sorting through them to find genuinely qualified AI engineers takes months. The best candidates have usually found something else by the time a decision is made.

This is the problem [Fonzi](https://fonzi.ai/) is built to fix.

## Meet Fonzi.ai: A Curated Talent Marketplace for AI Engineers

Fonzi is a curated talent marketplace that connects engineers with AI startups and high-growth tech companies. Founded in 2025 and based in Brooklyn, it focuses specifically on roles that traditional job boards don't serve well: ML Researchers, AI Research Scientists, ML Engineers, Data Scientists, Quant Researchers, AI Engineers, and Fullstack/Backend/Frontend Engineers.

The core idea is **quality over volume, on both sides.**

Candidates go through an application process, not just a sign-up. Fonzi reviews GitHub contributions, work histories, and portfolio projects, then runs a structured AI-powered screening interview before a human concierge recruiter reviews the profile. The goal is that every engineer in the pool is someone a top company would actually want to talk to.

Companies are vetted too. Fonzi only works with companies that are genuinely hiring: the listings are real, the roles are funded, and the hiring managers are engaged. Many listings name the company directly, which is a lot more transparent than the endless "stealth startup" postings on other platforms.

For candidates, the service is **completely free.** Fonzi earns a placement fee from companies only when a hire is made, so their incentives are aligned with actually getting you a job.

## Match Day: The Feature That Stood Out

The most distinctive thing about Fonzi is **Match Day**.

Once a month (and weekly starting March 2026), Fonzi runs a 48-hour hiring event. Engineers and companies are matched based on skills, salary expectations, company stage, and role fit. Companies review a curated shortlist and **submit salary-backed offers** to the candidates they want to interview.

Candidates receive multiple offers at once and choose who they actually want to speak with. No cold applications. No waiting weeks to hear back. No wondering whether your resume was even opened.

## What Makes Fonzi Different

A few things stand out compared to traditional job boards:

- **Pre-vetted on both sides**: Both engineers and companies go through a screening process, which raises the quality of every interaction.
- **No ghosting, guaranteed**: Every candidate gets a status update. If you're not selected, you get personalised feedback on why. That's genuinely rare in hiring today.
- **A dedicated Concierge Recruiter**: Every candidate has a real human contact who guides them through the process.
- **AI-powered fraud detection**: Fonzi reviews GitHub activity, portfolio projects, and work histories to verify candidates and flag inconsistencies.
- **Salary transparency upfront**: Companies submit salary-backed offers during Match Day, so you know what a role pays before committing to any interviews.
- **Discreet by design**: If you're currently employed and just exploring, the process is confidential.

There's also a rewards programme: **$100 for completing your first Match Day**, $50 for every first-round interview you complete, and a **$1,000 hire bonus** if you land a role and stay 90 days. Referral bonuses are available too.
