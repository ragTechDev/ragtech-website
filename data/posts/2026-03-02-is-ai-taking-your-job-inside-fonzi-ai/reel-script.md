# Reel Script: Is AI Taking Your Job?
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok
**Target length:** ~60-75 seconds
**Format:** Talking heads, three hosts, casual setting

---

## HOOK (0:00 - 0:08)

**[NATASHA — direct to camera, confident]**
Okay so. Is AI taking your job, or is it creating jobs?
Because honestly? Both answers are correct.

---

## THE TWO SIDES (0:08 - 0:25)

**[SALONI]**
Look, AI is already doing things we used to give to junior developers.
Data labelling, routine code reviews, basic customer support — it's just... faster and cheaper.
And with tools like Cursor and Copilot, one engineer is now doing the work of three.

**[VICTORIA]**
But at the same time, demand for AI engineers has gone up 25%.
Like, every single company right now needs someone to build, train, and deploy these systems.
ML Engineers, Data Scientists, AI Engineers — these roles are everywhere.

**[NATASHA]**
So the jobs exist. That's not really the problem.

---

## THE REAL PROBLEM (0:25 - 0:42)

**[SALONI]**
The problem is the job market itself is kind of broken right now.

**[VICTORIA]**
Like, did you know roughly 30% of job postings in 2026 are ghost jobs?
Companies post roles they're not even actively hiring for.
So you're applying, spending time, going through the process...

**[SALONI]**
...and the role was never real to begin with.

**[NATASHA]**
And even if you're genuinely good, right — strong GitHub, solid experience —
most job boards just filter you by keywords.
You could be an amazing engineer and just... not make it past the ATS.

---

## THE BRIDGE / SUBTLE FONZI PLACEMENT (0:42 - 1:00)

**[VICTORIA]**
So what we've been seeing is some platforms trying to fix this.
Like there's this one called Fonzi — they actually vet both the engineers AND the companies before matching them.

**[SALONI]**
And the cool part is they do this thing called Match Day.
Instead of you applying to 50 companies and hearing nothing back,
companies actually come to you with salary-backed offers.

**[NATASHA]**
In a 48-hour window. You just pick who you want to talk to.

**[VICTORIA]**
Which honestly sounds like how hiring should have worked all along.

---

## OUTRO / CTA (1:00 - 1:08)

**[NATASHA — to camera]**
We'll drop the link in bio if you want to check it out.
And let us know in the comments — do you think AI is taking jobs or creating them?
Because we genuinely want to know.

**[ALL THREE]**
This is ragTech. Bytes and banter. 👋

---

## Production Notes

- **Saloni** delivers the "jobs being replaced" angle — she's the skeptic
- **Victoria** is the optimist / data person on the "jobs being created" angle
- **Natasha** moderates and lands the transitions
- Fonzi mention should feel like a natural discovery, not a pitch ("we've been seeing...", "there's this one called...")
- Avoid reading from a script — use this as a teleprompter guide, keep it loose
- Consider B-roll during the Match Day description: screen recording of fonzi.ai / talent.fonzi.ai
