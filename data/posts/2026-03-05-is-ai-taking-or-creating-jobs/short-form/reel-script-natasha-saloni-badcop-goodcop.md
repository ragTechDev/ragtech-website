# Reel Script: Natasha + Saloni — Bad Cop / Good Cop
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok
**Hosts:** Natasha (bad cop) + Saloni (good cop)
**Target length:** ~75-90 seconds
**Format:** Back-and-forth, side by side or split screen

---

## SCRIPT

> Recorded separately, edited as one reel. Speaker labels are for recording reference only.

---

**[0:00 - 0:06 | HOOK]**

**NATASHA:** Is AI taking our jobs?

**SALONI:** Or is it creating them?

---

**[0:06 - 0:28 | THE TWO TAKES]**

**NATASHA:** I think AI is taking jobs. And I've seen it.
Junior devs, data labellers, code reviewers — AI tools are already doing that work faster and cheaper.
One engineer with Copilot today does the work of three.

**SALONI:** I think AI is creating jobs. Real ones.
LinkedIn data shows AI has already created over 1.3 million new roles.
But, and this is important, most of those are specialised AI roles.
Not every software dev can just pivot into that overnight.

**NATASHA:** And companies are laying people off because they THINK AI will replace them.
Not because it already has.
You're losing your job to a prediction, not a reality.

---

**[0:28 - 0:48 | WHERE THEY AGREE]**

**SALONI:** Okay but here's where it gets complicated.
Even if the jobs are there, the market is really noisy right now.

**NATASHA:** Nearly 1 in 3 employers admit to posting jobs they have no intention of filling.
Roles that aren't actually real.
You're applying, going through rounds...

**SALONI:** ...and the role was never real.

**NATASHA:** And even strong candidates get filtered out by keywords.
Your GitHub, your actual work, none of that cuts through on most platforms.

---

**[0:48 - 1:05 | TIP + FONZI]**

**SALONI:** So if you're job hunting right now, one thing that actually helps:
look for platforms where companies come to you with offers, not the other way around.
Some run structured hiring events where vetted companies review your profile and send salary-backed offers upfront.
You pick who you want to talk to. No ghosting. No 10-round loops.
[show fonzi.ai on screen]
Fonzi does exactly this — they call it Match Day.

**NATASHA:** And the difference is real.
One engineer spent seven months and 119 interviews doing it the old way.
With a process like this: signed offer in 2 to 3 weeks.

---

**[1:05 - 1:18 | CLOSE + CTA]**

**NATASHA:** So is AI taking jobs? Yeah, some of them.

**SALONI:** Is AI creating jobs? Also yes.

**NATASHA:** The real problem is the job market itself is broken.

**SALONI:** But there are smarter ways to navigate it. Link in bio.

**NATASHA:** And drop a comment — are you team "AI is taking jobs" or team "AI is creating them"?
We are clearly not on the same side.

## Production Notes

- **Recorded separately and independently** — neither needs to watch the other's footage first
- **Editor intercuts** the two scripts as alternating narrators on the same question
- **Saloni**: show `fonzi.ai` on screen clearly (top or middle, 100% visible) during the Match Day lines
- **CTAs are identical**: both end with the same question to camera — editor can cut them back to back as a closing beat
- **Tone for Natasha**: matter-of-fact, not dramatic. The point lands harder when she's calm about it.
- **Tone for Saloni**: confident and direct. She acknowledges the job market problem before Fonzi so the mention feels earned, not tacked on.
- Do NOT call Fonzi an "AI Recruiter" on screen or in caption
- Don't mention Fonzi in hashtags or caption
