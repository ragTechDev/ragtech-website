# Fonzi AI BRIEF

## Campaign Overview
Engineers don't need another job board. They need their dream job. Fonzi helps engineers skip
the nightmare of endless applications and land at companies where they'll build products they
love, with teams that respect their craft and their time.
IMPORTANT: Your post should drive engagement (commenting) about this new and scary
reality of today’s job market. So lean into that.
What is Fonzi?
Fonzi is a talent marketplace that matches elite engineers with AI startups and high-growth tech
companies through a high-signal, structured hiring process we call Match Day.

## How It Works:
1. Apply Once – Create a profile to be considered for multiple opportunities
2. Match Day – Bi-monthly Match Days where verified companies submit salary-backed
offers to candidates
3. Get Offers – Choose the best fit with transparent salaries upfront
4. Human Support – Dedicated recruiter guides you through your search
