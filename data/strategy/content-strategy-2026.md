- Big Tech insiders speaking from privilege
- Western-centric career advice (FAANG or bust)

ragTech's white space: **Honest, opinionated, Southeast Asia-rooted takes on tech careers and the industry — from people actually living it, not performing it.**

**For mid-career tech professionals in Southeast Asia**: We're the friends in tech who'll actually tell you what's going on -- honest takes on building a career, using AI intentionally, and finding financial independence without burning out.

**For global tech students and early-career developers**: We're the relatable voice that makes engineering culture hilarious and approachable -- showing that tech doesn't have to be serious all the time.

**Through-line**: Whether you're just starting out or already mid-career, we say the quiet part loud about AI, careers, money, and what it actually means to build a life in tech.

## 08. THE VIEWER-TO-BUYER JOURNEY

### The Funnel ragTech Is Building (Whether You Know It or Not)

```
DISCOVER → TRUST → BELONG → BUY
```

| Stage | Touchpoint | What They're Thinking | Your Job |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|----------|
| **Discover** | YouTube episode (algorithm or share) | "These people are saying what I've been thinking" | Make the first minute irresistible. Strong hook, clear POV, no slow intro. |
| **Explore** | Binge 2–3 more episodes / read a blog post | "They know what they're talking about" | Consistent quality and consistent voice — they're auditing you |
| **Trust** | Subscribe to newsletter | "I want more of this regularly" | Newsletter must deliver a distinct value from the podcast — not just recaps, but exclusive takes |
| **Belong** | Comment, share, respond to newsletter | "These are my people" | Acknowledge replies. Feature community. Make them feel seen. |
| **Buy** | Purchase Techie Taboo / join community / attend event | "I want to support this and get more access" | Clear, low-friction offers. Never more than one CTA at a time. |

### The Current Gap

Right now the journey **breaks at "Belong"**. You have Discover (YouTube) and partial Trust (newsletter exists), but there is no **belonging layer** — no place where the audience becomes a community, not just a viewership. This is what causes people to watch passively for two years without converting.

The fix is not expensive: a free Discord, a dedicated community hashtag, or a "listener spotlight" segment in episodes creates the belonging moment that converts lurkers into loyalists.

### CTA Architecture (Per Platform)

| Platform | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| YouTube description | Subscribe to newsletter | Check Techie Taboo |
| Blog post footer | Subscribe to newsletter | Listen to related episode |
| Newsletter | — | Buy / Join / Attend (one active offer) |
| Podcast episode outro | "Link in description to newsletter" | Current product offer |

One rule: **one CTA per piece of content.** The mistake is mentioning everything and converting nobody.
