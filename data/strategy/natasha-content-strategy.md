## HOOK OPTIMIZATION

**Proven Hook Formulas**:
- "You've been punked by..."
- "Why everyone is wrong about..."
- "When [relatable situation] but then [technical twist]"
- "The dangerous myth of..."
- "What [X] doesn't want you to know about..."

**Avoid**:
- "How to..." (tutorial format underperforms)
- "What is..." (explainer-only format low reach)
- "Let me explain..." (too educational)
