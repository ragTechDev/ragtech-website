# ragTech Podcast Pitch Script - Impact Award (5 Minutes)

**Speaker**: Any ragTech co-host
**Tone**: Conversational, direct, authentic
**Style**: ragTech brand voice - first-person plural, accessible, honest
**Focus**: Social impact and community building in tech

---

## **INTRODUCTION (0:00 - 0:45)**

In Singapore and across Southeast Asia, we have two problems happening at once.

Thousands of tech professionals are building the future in isolation - navigating AI anxiety, career uncertainty, and financial pressure without honest guidance. At the same time, millions of non-tech people are affected by technology every day but have no idea how it works or how it's changing their lives.

We're ragTech, and we're creating the bridge between these two worlds - the community that both tech professionals and curious non-techies desperately need.

We're three women building careers in Singapore's tech scene - Natasha, Saloni, and Victoria - and we started a podcast to say the quiet part out loud about what technology actually means for everyone building a life in the modern world. We're not a women-in-tech charity narrative - we're women in tech who happen to have strong opinions, and that framing matters. It shows younger women that you can be in tech and have strong, unfiltered takes. It changes the image of what women in tech sound like.

---

## **THE PROBLEM (0:45 - 1:30)**

The tech content landscape isn't just broken - it's creating real harm for both audiences.

For tech professionals, engineers in Southeast Asia face unique challenges that Western content creators ignore. Regional salary differences, local tech policies, cultural expectations around career progression. Yet most available content assumes FAANG salaries and Silicon Valley work culture.

For non-tech people, the problem is even worse. They're making decisions about AI tools, privacy settings, digital payments, and screen time for their kids - but most tech content is either too technical or too hyped to be useful. They need to understand how technology affects their jobs, their finances, their families, but have nowhere to turn for honest, accessible explanations.

This creates two dangerous knowledge gaps. Tech professionals are making career decisions based on advice that doesn't apply to their reality. Non-tech people are making life decisions about technology without understanding the real implications.

The impact? For tech professionals: burnout, career stagnation, and talented people leaving the industry. For everyone else: anxiety about AI, privacy violations, bad financial decisions, and feeling left behind by the digital world.

Our audiences aren't just consumers of content. They're people making critical life decisions with incomplete information - software engineers navigating career transitions, parents deciding about AI in education, small business owners choosing tech tools, students wondering if tech is right for them.

---

## **OUR SOLUTION (1:30 - 2:30)**

We're building the knowledge infrastructure that both tech professionals and curious non-techies desperately need.

Our podcast creates spaces for honest conversations that work for both audiences. For tech professionals, we talk about the human side of building a career in technology. For non-tech people, we translate complex tech topics into real-world impact.

Like our most popular episode - "Is your favorite AI app just a wrapper?" - 1,500 engineers watched that because we're addressing AI anxiety with skepticism and clarity. But hundreds of non-tech listeners also tuned in because we explained how AI wrappers actually affect their daily apps and privacy. This isn't just content - it's mental health support for an entire generation, and clarity for everyone else.

When we talk about "Engineer retired early with Bitcoin," we're not just sharing a career story. We're explaining cryptocurrency in a way that helps both tech professionals planning their finances AND non-tech people wondering if they should invest. That episode got the most likes because someone finally talked about money and tech in a way that made sense for regular people.

We're creating five critical knowledge areas that serve both audiences:

AI literacy without the hype - helping tech professionals make career decisions AND non-tech people understand AI's real impact on their lives
Career navigation that respects local contexts - challenging the one-size-fits-all ladder for engineers AND showing non-tech people what tech careers actually look like
Financial planning for regional realities - independence paths for Southeast Asian tech professionals AND investment clarity for everyone else
Building while employed - supporting the creator economy within tech AND explaining the gig economy to curious outsiders
Tech and society connections - helping engineers understand their civic impact AND showing everyone how technology shapes our world

## **DELIVERY NOTES**

**Pacing**: Conversational, not rushed. Use natural pauses.
**Energy**: Warm but direct, like talking to colleagues.
**Key phrases to emphasize**:
- "knowledge infrastructure"
- "Southeast Asia's tech ecosystem"
- "knowledge equity"
- "community building"
- "say the quiet part loud"

**Impact-focused delivery**:
- Speak with conviction about the social problems being solved
- Use pauses to let impact statements land
- Maintain warmth while emphasizing the serious nature of the knowledge gap
- Show passion for the community-building mission

**Avoid**:
- Corporate buzzwords
- AI hype language
- Western tech clichés
- Tutorial-style explanations
- Overly casual tone (this is for an impact award panel)

**Brand voice consistency**: Use "we", "us", "our" throughout. Keep sentences short and direct. No em dashes - use commas or restructure.

---

*Script length: Approximately 5 minutes when delivered at natural speaking pace*
